Built from departments

Turn on what you use. Nothing more.

Each feature is a department you switch on in Settings. Your team sees only what applies to them, by role.

  1. Order
  2. Kitchen
  3. Checkout
  4. Inventory
  5. Cash-up

Sales

  • Checkout with cash, card or QR/SPEI
  • Tables, orders and a timer per table
  • Kitchen display (KDS) connected to checkout
  • Promotions and reservations
  • Cash closings, cash in and cash out

Inventory

  • Products and inventory
  • Barcodes and scales for bulk goods
  • Purchasing and suppliers
  • Batches and expiration dates
  • Recipes and costing; size and color variants

Customers

  • Customers and loyalty program
  • Customer credit
  • Quotes and layaways
  • Appointments for salons, spas and repair shops
  • Digital tickets

Management

  • Dashboards, reports and executive summaries
  • Demand forecasting and peak hours
  • Staff, roles and PIN permissions
  • CFDI 4.0 e-invoicing
  • Full database backups
