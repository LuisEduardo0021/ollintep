Precision diagnostics in seconds

Optimized for repair shops, technical service centers and mobile refurbishing companies.

ADB connection without root

Complete inspection over USB without voiding warranties or requiring superuser rights. Instant automatic detection when the phone is plugged in.

Battery health and cycles

Real reading of charge cycles, health percentage, operating temperature, instant voltage and the cell's chemical degradation rate.

CPU, memory and storage

Precise identification of the SoC, active cores, RAM status (free/swap) and internal NAND health (sectors and speed).

Display and touch sensors

Interactive touch digitizer tests, dead zone detection, RGB color checks and a proximity and light sensor matrix.

Automatic companion probe

The “Diagmind Probe” companion app deploys over USB for deep real-time telemetry and active peripheral tests with no manual setup.

Local AI assistant

Correlates system logs (logcat) and hardware metrics using AI that runs 100% on the technician's computer, without sending data to the internet.
